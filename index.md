I am a senior CNRS researcher in ecology at ISEM, the [Institute of Evolution at Montpellier University](https://isem-evolution.fr){.plain} in France. I received my PhD in theoretical ecology from Wageningen University.

I am interested in ecological stability, tipping points and the effects of eco-evolutionary feedbacks on ecosystem resilience under global change. I develop and test indicators of resilience (early-warning signals) in a wide range of systems, from ecology and biology to climate and psychology.

I am an active member of [SPARCS](https://sparcs-center.org){.plain} (the Synergy Program for Analyzing Resilience and Critical Transitions) and of [Open Scholar](https://openscholar.info){.plain}, a scholar-governed model for academic publication and evaluation.

Since 2025, I have served as Representative (Editor-in-Chief) of [Peer Community in Ecology](https://ecology.peercommunityin.org){.plain} (PCI Ecology), a non-profit open-science organization of scientists that evaluates, recommends and publishes research preprints in free open access.
