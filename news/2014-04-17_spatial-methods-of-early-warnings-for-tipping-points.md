Recently our [paper on spatial indicators for critical transitions](http://www.plosone.org/article/info%3Adoi%2F10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0092097) was published in PloS One. In this paper we summarize methods and create a flowchart for looking for indicators of upcoming transitions in spatial data. It is a natural follow-up paper from our [previous work](http://www.plosone.org/article/info%3Adoi%2F10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0041010#s5 "PloS One paper on Methods for Estimating Early-Warnings is out!") on methods for timeseries. The methods of the paper are now summarized in the[ spatial indicators](http://www.early-warning-signals.org/?page_id=15 "Spatial indicators") section of the EWS toolbox website together with the [actual R code](https://github.com/earlywarningtoolbox/spatial_warnings).
