![](/images/2012/09/knaw11.jpg "KNAW1")Our [colloquium](http://www.knaw.nl/Pages/DEF/32/060.bGFuZz1OTA.html) and [masterclass](http://www.knaw.nl/Pages/DEF/33/184.bGFuZz1OTA.html) on ‘Early-warning signals for critical transitions: bridging the gap between theory and practice’ will be hosted by the Dutch Royal Science and Arts Society (KNAW) from 10 to 12 of October 2012 in Amsterdam. This is also going to be the official kick-off of [SparcS](http://www.sparcs-center.org/) – the Synergy Program for Analyzing Resilience and Critical transitionS: an initiative on getting people to work on issues of critical transitions and resilience in a broad range of scientific fields.
