Our submission of an interactive visualization version of the [earlywarnings](http://www.early-warning-signals.org/?page_id=81) R package for critical transitions in the Data Challenge competition organised by the [Waterloo Institute for Complexity and Innovation](http://wici.ca/new/) got into the 4 [finalists](http://wici.ca/new/resources/wici-data-challenge-2/). Although we didn’t get the first place, the judges were very flattering and Leo Lahti and myself are really proud to have made it that far in a short time. We hope that this will be a kick into developing more the package and making it more interactive and available. You can read more on it [here](http://issuu.com/waterloo_institute_complexity/docs/dakos_lahti_earlywarnings_main/1?e=3331502/3978780).
