![](http://ccs2018.web.auth.gr/sites/default/files/banner/seasidelogo.jpg)There is still time to submit your abstract in our satellite session on [***Robustness, Adaptability and Critical Transitions in Living Systems***](https://easychair.org/cfp/ReACT3) during the upcoming conference of the [Complex Systems Society in Thessaloniki](http://ccs2018.web.auth.gr/), Greece from the 23-28 of September 2018.
