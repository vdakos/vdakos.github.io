Just a week before the [resilience conference](http://www.resilience2014.org) 2014 in Montpellier a short brochure of our forthcoming book on “Principles for Building Resilience: Sustaining Ecosystem Services in Social-Ecological Systems” (Cambridge University Press, 2014) has become available by the [Stockholm Resilience Center](http://www.stockholmresilience.org/21/research/research-news/4-22-2014-applying-resilience-thinking.html). The brochure summarizes the expanded treatment of resilience principles as we have discussed in an earlier paper “[Towards principles for enhancing the resilience of ecosystem services](/news/2012-10-18_7-principles-for-enhancing-the-resilience-of-ecosystem-services.md "7 principles for enhancing the resilience of ecosystem services")“.
