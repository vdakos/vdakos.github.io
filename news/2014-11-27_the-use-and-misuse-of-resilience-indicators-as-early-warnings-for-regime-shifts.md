Regime shifts have been a long sought theme of research in marine ecosystems. Controversies, new methods and alternative hypotheses on how to study and understand such marine regime shifts are summarized in the special issue of the Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society on [Marine regime shifts around the globe: theory, drivers and impacts](http://rstb.royalsocietypublishing.org/content/370/1659) that just appeared online. Thus, we couldn’t think of a better place to publish a review-research paper on the [use and misuse of resilience indicators as early warnings](http://rstb.royalsocietypublishing.org/content/370/1659/20130263) for regime shifts in marine but not only systems.
