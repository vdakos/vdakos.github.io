Our long lasting project with the [RAYS](https://www.facebook.com/pages/RAYS/314948370436) cohort just got appeared in a review/synthesis [paper](http://www.annualreviews.org/doi/abs/10.1146/annurev-environ-051211-123836) where we try to summarize basic principles that so far have been widely proposed to be fundamental for supporting the resilience of ecosystem services. Important part of this work is that we try to identify gaps in the existing research on resilience factors based on literature and expert opinion. This effort received quite some enthusiasm so that we decided to extent it into a book.
