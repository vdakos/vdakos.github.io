A first joint work in my new [lab of Jordie Bascompte](http://www.bascompte.net) got published in [Nature Communications](http://www.nature.com/ncomms/2013/130815/ncomms3350/full/ncomms3350.html) few weeks ago. By simulating mutualistic communities of 57 empirical plant pollinator and plant seed-disperser networks we identify scenarios where species’ tolerance is extremely sensitive to the direction of change in the strength of mutualistic interaction, as well as to the observed mutualistic trade-offs between the number of partners and the strength of the interactions.
