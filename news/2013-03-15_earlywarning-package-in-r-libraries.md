[![logoEWS](/images/2013/03/logoews1.png)](https://github.com/earlywarningtoolbox) Together with Leo Lahti, we fixed bugs and moved the [earlywarnings toolbox](http://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/earlywarnings/index.html) in *R.* It is now a library ready to be installed from your preferable *CRAN* repository. In the process, we also migrated the earlywarnings toolbox to [github](http://github.com/earlywarningtoolbox) for shifitng towards open-source, community-based project development. We hope this will facilitate the use of the toolbox both for research and education. More details on the [Early Warning Signals Toolbox webpage](http://www.early-warning-signals.org).
