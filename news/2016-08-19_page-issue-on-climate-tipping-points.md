![PAGES](/images/pages-mag-2016-1-cvr-212x300.jpg)In the just published [PAGES issue](http://pastglobalchanges.org/products/pages-magazine/7018-24-1-tipping-points) of Past Global Changes there is a science highlight on [Tipping points](http://pastglobalchanges.org/download/docs/magazine/2016-1/PAGESmagazine_2016(1)_Full_LowREs.pdf) or “Lessons from the Past for the Future” as the editorial suggests. In ten short 2-page contributions the most up-to-date ideas about past climatic transitions are highlighted together with examples from ecological and socio-ecological abrupt shifts. We have contributed in this issue a short piece on how the [shift of the Sahara](http://pastglobalchanges.org/download/docs/magazine/2016-1/PAGESmagazine_2016%281%29_20-21_Bathiany.pdf) has been shaping our thinking about abrupt change.
