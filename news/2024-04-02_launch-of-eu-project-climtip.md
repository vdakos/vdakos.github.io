We are participating in the EU Horizon funded [ClimTip](https://www.climate-tipping-points.eu/) project that kicked-off earlier in March 2023. The project aims to quantify the impacts of climate tipping points on Earth systems resilience. Our participation will focus on the repercussions of climate tipping events on the on ecosystems and biodiversity.
