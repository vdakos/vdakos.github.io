The [paper](http://www.plosone.org/article/info%3Adoi%2F10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0038410) we were working on within the EUR-Oceans group for the Baltic Sea just appeared in PLoS One. We show for the first time how different methods for identifying regime shifts can be applied in the marine environment and how they can be interpreted. Hopefully this study will pave the way for same sort of applications in coastal and marine ecosystems.
